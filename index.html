📘 Circle Geometry & Measure Reference

1. Standard Circle Equation: $(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 = r^2$
Centre is $(a, b)$ and radius is $r$. Complete the square when given $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$.
2. Circle Theorems in Coordinate Geometry
• Tangent $\perp$ Radius ($m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1$)
• Angle in a semicircle $= 90^\circ$
• Perpendicular from centre to a chord bisects the chord.
3. Arc Length & Sector Area ($\theta$ in radians)
Arc length: $s = r\theta$  |  Sector Area: $A_{\text{sector}} = \dfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta$  |  ($180^\circ = \pi\text{ rad}$)
4. Area of a Circular Segment ($\theta$ in radians)
$A_{\text{segment}} = A_{\text{sector}} - A_{\triangle} = \dfrac{1}{2}r^2(\theta - \sin\theta)$
